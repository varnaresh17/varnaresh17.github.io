INTERPRET / INFRARED SPECTROSCOPY

From bands to possible bonds.

Load a spectrum, examine its bands, and explore candidate functional groups. Your files stay in this browser.

1 Add data or an image2 Detect or mark bands3 Review possible assignments

Upload your spectrum data

Use CSV, TXT, TSV, or DAT exported from your instrument. The x-axis must be wavenumber in cm⁻¹; choose the signal type below.

Or paste numerical data

Detection uses a moving average and local prominence within ±500 cm⁻¹. %T / T is converted to absorbance for detection; the plotted signal keeps your selected units. No automatic baseline fit or peak deconvolution is applied.

Interpret a spectrum image

Upload a PNG, JPEG, or WebP graph. Calibrate two known x-axis ticks, then click absorption bands on the trace. For transmittance, click the downward troughs.

Choose an image to begin. Image positions are approximate and depend on calibration and resolution.

This mode uses your clicked positions; it does not claim automatic image recognition. Exported numerical data is preferable for precise band detection. Proprietary instrument files and PDF graphs should first be exported to CSV or a supported image.

Already know the band positions?

Enter wavenumbers in cm⁻¹, separated by commas, spaces, or new lines. This gives candidate assignments without a numerical spectrum.
