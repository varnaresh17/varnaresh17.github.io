Scientific Tools & Calculators

From measured data to meaningful results...

Free online tools for XRD d-spacing and crystallinity, FTIR spectrum interpretation, CIE color coordinates and correlated color temperature (CCT), lighting metrics, and PLQY/PLQE. Choose a workspace to enter values, upload spectra, review results, and export your analysis.

01 / STRUCTUREXRD

nλ = 2d sinθ

XRD: d-spacing & crystallinity

Connect diffraction angle and interplanar spacing, and estimate crystallinity from integrated peak areas.

  • Bragg’s law and d-spacing
  • Angle, wavelength and diffraction order
  • Crystalline area Ac, total area At and crystallinity (%)
  • Visual guide to crystalline peaks and the amorphous background

Input: diffraction values or integrated areas.

02 / CHEMICAL BONDSFTIR

Bands → candidate groups

FTIR spectrum interpretation

Explore absorption bands and possible functional-group assignments with your spectrum or a calibrated image.

  • Upload numerical data or paste a spectrum table
  • Detect bands with adjustable peak settings
  • Calibrate an uploaded spectrum image and mark bands
  • Review candidate assignments and export results

Input: numerical spectrum, spectrum image, or peak list.

Open FTIR explorer Assignments are candidates to assess with sample chemistry and complementary evidence.

CIE color coordinates & CCT

Locate emission color, compare display gamut, and evaluate light quality, efficiency and biological weighting.

  • CIE 1931 and 1976 diagrams; coordinates and spectra
  • CCT, Duv, white points, color purity and RGB gamut
  • CRI / R9 / R15, CQS and TM-30 Rf / Rg
  • LED efficiency, white-light efficacy and photobiological quantities

Input: coordinates, emission spectra, or measurement values.

PLQY & PLQE calculators

Calculate sample absorption and quantum yield using the measurements and normalization appropriate to your experiment.

  • Integrating-sphere photon balance using S₀, S₁ and S₂
  • Sample absorption, external and internal quantum yield (%)
  • Relative standards and dilution-series fitting
  • Energy yield, Stokes shift, uncertainty and lifetime-derived rates

Input: blank / sample spectra, integrated values, or reference-standard data.
