Naresh Varnakavi, Ph.D.

Philadelphia, PA · varna.naresh@gmail.com
LinkedIn · Google Scholar · ORCID

Professional Summary

Experimental materials scientist with a Ph.D. in photonic glasses and multidisciplinary postdoctoral research experience in nanomaterial synthesis, surface engineering, and optical characterization. Expertise spans the synthesis of carbide, carbonitride, and nitride MAX phases and MXenes; CsPbX3 quantum dots and nanoplatelets; lead-free and double-perovskite nanocrystals; 0D@3D and 2D@3D heterostructures; and up-conversion nanoparticle@perovskite composites. Author of 39 journal articles and 8 proceedings papers, including Nature Communications and Advanced Functional Materials, and co-inventor on a U.S. provisional patent. Experienced in developing reproducible, parameter-controlled synthesis and spectroscopy workflows that can support automated experimentation and data-guided materials optimization.
